## Tracer gas detector (Sniffer430) - name never on slides
- Locates even the smallest leaks in underground, underfloor and in-wall pipes (.com).
- Locate leaks in 30 minutes or less, even 10 ft deep or under concrete slab (.co).
- No training needed: turn it on and follow the tracer gas to the leak source; works much like a metal detector (.co).
- Minimize damage and keep clients happy: find the leak before breaking ground; reduces guesswork (.co).
- Customers report detections over 10 ft deep, over 14 ft in the FAQ, and through multiple feet of concrete slab (.co). Penetration 9-10 ft of soil and several inches of concrete; 3-4 m underground (.com).
- Gas: 5% hydrogen / 95% nitrogen; non-toxic, non-flammable, eco-friendly; tiny hydrogen molecules penetrate dense materials (wood, ceramic tile, flooring) (.co).
- PRESSURE (corrected by the company, 2026-11-07; the .co FAQ's "20-25 psi" is not accurate): test pressure is typically 1.5-4 bar (about 20-60 psi), up to 5 bar if needed. Stay within the line's normal working pressure so the test causes no damage; go higher only when needed. Works with water in the line (no draining) (.co).
- Any pipe material: copper, PVC, galvanized, polyethylene, cast iron (.co). Finds even the smallest pinhole leaks; detects small leaks that make no turbulent flow/sound (.co, .com).
- Tracer gas is the most accurate, responsive and reliable of the three methods (.co FAQ).
- vs thermal: thermal shows where water accumulated, not necessarily the leak; not reliable in wet areas or on reflective tiles; tracer gas needs no line of sight, e.g. behind a kitchen cabinet (.com, .co).
- Hardware: wireless sensor (move freely), 7-inch touchscreen with real-time readings, light and ergonomic, sensor stands on its own, adjustable padded neck strap, flexible rubber cone that seals on surfaces, audible alert that beeps faster as readings rise (.co).
- 1 ppm hydrogen resolution, readings update every second, Bluetooth about 10 m (.com specs).
- In the box: screen unit, sensor, dual charger, silicone bumper case, neck strap, hard case with custom-fit padding, user manual (.co).
- No calibration, no filter changes, no service plan; keep it clean and dry (.co FAQ).
- Thick slabs: 30+ psi, cone flat for a few seconds, confirm with a small pilot hole (.co FAQ).
- Applications: underground (soil, grass, stone paving), underfloor, in-wall, pools and spas; any system: hot and cold water, steam, propane/butane, oil and fuel (.co, .com).
